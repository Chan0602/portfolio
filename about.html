Outside of design

Outside of design, I’m usually baking, taking photos, or collecting cute cat pictures. These little hobbies shape the way I see and make things. Baking teaches me patience and process. Photography helps me notice light, composition, and emotion. Cats remind me that small moments can bring a lot of joy.

Baking is one of the ways I create for people I care about. I love hosting, making cakes for friends’ birthdays, and turning small celebrations into something warmer and more personal. I like the process of shaping something from scratch and sharing it with others.

Photography has been something I’ve loved since I was little. I enjoy seeing the world through a viewfinder, where ordinary moments can suddenly feel more intentional, quiet, and beautiful. It taught me to frame, observe, and find meaning in the details.

I’ve never had a cat of my own, but many cats have kept me company for a while. They remind me that small moments can feel deeply comforting.
