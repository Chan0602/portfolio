What is Dandi?

Dandi is a speculative bio-smart wearable and companion app that makes invisible health signals visible, translating biometrics into clear patterns and actionable insights.

My Role

As part of a small product team, I contributed to research, concept development, interaction design, and prototyping from low-fidelity flows to a polished experience.

Context

Dandi reframes health tracking as a calm, emotionally intelligent ritual - not a clinical dashboard.

The prompt was to design a speculative tool that tracks something intangible about the human sensory experience and supports wellness or behavior change.

Impact / award image

Where We Began

She had always had irregular periods, stubborn acne, and a gut feeling something was wrong. For years she assumed, "This is just how my body is."

Origin story motion

It was not until a routine checkup that a doctor mentioned PCOS almost in passing, but she still had no real understanding of what it meant.

This personal story became the foundation for the product direction.

What We Found

Health data only becomes meaningful when people can understand what their body is trying to say.

The opportunity was to help users notice patterns without making them feel reduced to numbers.

Solution

Visual Identity

An earthy palette, soft floral motifs, and gentle motion create a product language that feels closer to self-care than a medical dashboard.

The Ear Cuff

A discreet wearable component makes the concept feel fashionable enough to wear daily and functional enough to support continuous biosensing.

Feature video

Feature #2: See your whole health in one place

Feature video

Key signals and cycle patterns are framed as a morning check-in rather than a dense medical report.

What's Next

Validate, expand, and scale.

The next step is clinical validation, followed by pilot testing with users and expansion into other conditions where the body sends continuous but difficult-to-interpret signals.

Key Takeaway

Data means nothing without clarity.

The hardest part is not only collecting signals, but making them understandable, emotionally safe, and useful in daily life.
